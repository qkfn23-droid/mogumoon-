What is Zodiac Compatibility?

Zodiac compatibility analyzes the harmony between two people by comparing their sun signs. It interprets compatibility based on the elements (fire, earth, air, water) and modalities (cardinal, fixed, mutable) of the 12 zodiac signs.

Como usar

1. Enter both people's birth dates.
2. Each person's zodiac sign and decan are displayed.
3. Check element, modality, and overall compatibility.

Perguntas frequentes

Is zodiac compatibility accurate?

Zodiac compatibility is for fun and reference. Real relationships depend more on effort and communication.

What is a decan?

Each zodiac sign divided into three 10-day periods. Even within the same sign, characteristics differ by decan.

Which signs go well together?

Generally same elements (fire-fire, earth-earth) match well. But complementary relationships can also be good.

Meus dados sao armazenados?

Nao. Todo o processamento ocorre apenas no seu navegador.

What is a Decan?

The decan system, originating in ancient Egypt, is the oldest astrological classification dividing the zodiac into 36 sections (10° each). Each decan has a planetary ruler and unique image. Picatrix (Ghāyat al-Ḥakīm, 11th century) and Cornelius Agrippa (De Occulta Philosophia, 16th century) are the most important traditional sources, with roots tracing back to Egyptian coffin texts circa 2100 BCE.

Perguntas frequentes

Como isso difere do zodíaco ocidental?

O zodíaco ocidental tem 12 signos (30° cada), decanos têm 36 (10° cada) — 3 vezes mais preciso.

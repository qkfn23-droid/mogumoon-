Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2025

1. Information We Collect

MOGU Moon (mogumoon.com) collects minimal personal information.

  • Automatically collected: Anonymous statistical data such as page views, visit duration, browser type, and country/region through Google Analytics.
  • User input: Birth date, time, and location entered for chart generation are processed only in your browser and never stored on any server.

2. How We Use Information

  • Website usage analysis and service improvement
  • Providing astrology charts and interpretations (processed in browser)

3. Information Sharing

MOGU Moon does not sell, trade, or share personal information with third parties. However, anonymized statistical data may be shared with Google Analytics and advertising providers (Google AdSense).

4. Cookies

This site uses the following cookies:

  • Google Analytics cookies: Visitor statistics
  • Theme cookies: Dark/light mode preference (localStorage)

5. Data Security

All astrology calculations are processed locally in your browser. No personal data is transmitted to servers or stored in databases.

6. Children

This site does not intentionally collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated and changes will be posted on this page.

What is Egyptian Decan?

Egyptian Decan astrology is a star system that began in ancient Egypt. It divides the year into 36 decans (10-day periods), each with its own guardian deity and characteristics. Decan charts were precisely drawn on pyramid tomb ceilings.

Questions frequentes

What is a decan?

An ancient Egyptian star system that divides the year into 36 periods of 10 days each.

Is it different from Western zodiac?

Yes, Western uses 12 signs (30 days each), while Egyptian decans use 36 (10 days each), providing more detail.

What is Egyptian decan's history?

It began about 4500 years ago in ancient Egypt. Decan charts have been found on pyramid tomb ceilings.

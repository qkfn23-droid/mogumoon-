What is Zi Wei Dou Shu?

Zi Wei Dou Shu is a divination system believed to have been created during the Song Dynasty in China, about 1,000 years ago. It places stars across 12 palaces to analyze different areas of life. With the Purple Star (Zi Wei) at the center, over 100 stars are used, forming a unique system distinct from Four Pillars (BaZi).

How to Use

2. Your Zi Wei Dou Shu chart will be automatically generated.
3. Review the star placements and interpretations across the 12 palaces.

FAQ

What is the difference between Zi Wei Dou Shu and BaZi?

BaZi uses the Five Elements and Heavenly Stems/Earthly Branches, while Zi Wei Dou Shu places stars in palaces. Both use birth data but analyze it very differently.

What is the history of Zi Wei Dou Shu?

It is said to have been created by Chen Xiyi during the Song Dynasty, approximately 1,000 years ago.

Is birth time required?

Yes, the chart changes significantly based on birth time. Please enter an accurate birth time if possible.

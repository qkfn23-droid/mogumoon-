What is a Crystal Ball?

The origins of crystal ball divination trace back to ancient Mesopotamia. Sumerian diviners known as baru gazed into water or polished stones to see visions. It later evolved into crystal spheres in medieval Europe. Today, it is used as a light-hearted decision-making or entertainment tool. It is best suited for Yes/No questions.

How to Use

1. Think of a question in your mind.
2. Type your question and touch the crystal ball.
3. The crystal ball shakes and reveals an answer.

FAQ

Are the answers accurate?

The crystal ball is for fun and inspiration. Make important decisions carefully.

Can I ask the same question multiple times?

Yes, you can ask multiple times. However, you may get different answers each time.

How old is crystal ball divination?

Crystal ball divination traces back to ancient Mesopotamia (around 2000 BCE). Sumerian diviners gazed into water or polished stones to see visions.

Is my data stored?

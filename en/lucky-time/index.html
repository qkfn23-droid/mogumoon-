What is Lucky Time?

Lucky time is an auspicious time period calculated by analyzing the relationship between a planet's ecliptic longitude and a sign's Domicile Lord in traditional astrology. Since ancient Greek and Roman times, each planet has been assigned a time period , and the hour when the planet ruling your sign is strongest becomes your lucky time. The Planetary Hours system follows the Chaldean order of Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, and Moon.

How Lucky Color Works

In traditional astrology, each planet is assigned a unique color. The Sun is gold, the Moon is silver/white, Mars is red, Venus is green, Jupiter is blue/purple, Saturn is black/brown, and Mercury is yellow/orange. The color of the planet most favorable to your sign on that day becomes your lucky color. This system is based on the Planetary Correspondences of Hellenistic astrology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does lucky time change every day?

Yes, since planets move every day, lucky time also changes daily.

Which planets are used for the calculation?

The 7 traditional planets are used: Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn.

What should I do during lucky time?

Scheduling important decisions, meetings, and new beginnings during lucky time is traditionally considered auspicious.

What is Birthday Compatibility?

Birthday compatibility analyzes the harmony between two people by comparing their birth dates. It checks how two people's energies interact from multiple perspectives including zodiac, numerology, and elemental compatibility.

How to Use

1. Enter both people's birth dates.
2. Compatibility analysis results are displayed.
3. Check interpretations from various perspectives.

FAQ

Are compatibility results accurate?

Compatibility is for fun and reference. Real relationships depend more on effort and communication.

Can I use it for friends too?

Yes, it can be used for lovers, friends, family, and all relationships.

What factors are analyzed?

Zodiac, numerological, and elemental (fire, earth, air, water) compatibility and more.

Is my data stored?

No. All processing happens in your browser only.

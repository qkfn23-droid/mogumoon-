Barley Receipts

Babylonian clay tablets have a fascinating record.

Planetary positions are written next to barley prices.

Barley prices, weather, planetary positions — on the same clay tablet.

They wrote planets on receipts 'ㅅ'

600 Years of Records

Babylonians recorded the sky every single day for about 600 years.

Planetary positions, weather, commodity prices, historical events.

Going Deeper

The history of astrology shows a fascinating pattern.

Sumer — barley harvests, weather, floods. They read nature.

Barley → King → Individual → Soul.

Cuneiform and Astrology

Sumerian cuneiform has something fascinating.

For example, combining "head" with "mouth" makes "to drink."

But is it "to drink"? Could it be "to eat"? Could "head + mouth" be "face + mouth" instead?

One symbol can have multiple meanings.

Couldn't astrology be the same? Saturn doesn't have just one interpretation..?

Astrology came from cuneiform. Could they be the same system 'ㅅ'?

Moon = curves inside a diamond..

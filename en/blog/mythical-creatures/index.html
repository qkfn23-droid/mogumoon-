Are Mythical Creatures Really Imaginary?

(Everything here is my personal opinion. If you disagree, your opinion is valid 'ㅅ')

Dragons, unicorns, griffins.

We call these "mythical creatures."

But I have a question.

Can You Imagine from Zero?

For a person to imagine and create something from absolute zero, with no seed at all, is extremely difficult.

Everything we draw, make, and imagine starts from something we've seen or felt.

Yet ancient people drew dragons. They drew phoenixes. Winged creatures, horned horses, fire-breathing reptiles.

They drew all this purely from imagination without seeing anything 'ㅅ'?

Were they geniuses ㅇ0ㅇ....!?

And here's another fun thing... these records keep appearing in modern movies, novels, and comics. Even today.

Ancient people imagined something this incredible... hmm 'ㅅ'a...

Historical Records

The interesting thing is that this isn't just one region's story.

The East has dragons. The West has dragons. Civilizations far apart from each other recorded similar creatures.

What If They Were Real?

If those creatures actually existed — where did they go?

Imagining freely, I personally think they might have been residents of other planets.

Think about it — if we earthlings went to another planet, from that planet's perspective, we would be the aliens.

Conversely, if beings from another planet had come to Earth — ancient people might have seen them as "mythical creatures."

The Mystery of Ancient Murals

What fascinates me the most are the figures drawn in ancient murals.

Beings that don't look human are depicted on those walls.

And there's something even more fascinating.

In ancient times, there was no established mathematical system like Arabic numerals. Yet people thousands of years ago knew mathematics and were observing the universe.

With no equipment at all 'ㅅ'?

Or did they have advanced technology like we do today?

Now here's where someone might say:

Ancient murals are lies!! They're fake! Made up!!

If that's really the case.. I give a thumbs up to the people who created such elaborate fakes 'ㅅ'b

That's impressive in a whole different way...

Ancient Egyptian Tombs

And what's even more amazing are ancient Egyptian tombs.

The planetary angles painted on the ceilings are astonishingly precise.

With the naked eye? By hand? Thousands of years ago?

So Aliens Are Real Then?

That, I don't know either 'ㅅ'v

It's just fascinating. Truly fascinating 'ㅅ'

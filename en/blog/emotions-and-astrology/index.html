Emotions and Astrology

(Everything here is my personal opinion. If you disagree, you're right 'ㅅ')

Earlier I said astrology is math. Everything is numbers.

But one thing bugs me. What about emotions?

Machines calculate. Put numbers in, answers come out. But machines don't know love. They don't get butterflies. They don't miss anyone. They don't get jealous.

If you ask me what's the biggest difference between humans and machines — I'd say emotions.

So a question arises. If astrology is math, are emotions math too?

Your spouse is written in your birth chart

This is my favorite part of astrology. Love. Spouse fortune.

Look at a birth chart and it tells you what kind of person your spouse will be. Personality, timing of meeting, even appearance hints. Different for every person.

In a birth chart made of numbers — love, which belongs to the realm of emotions, is written there.

Is this math? Emotions? Both?

But I think love is a fantasy. Love in reality gets tangled up in all sorts of situations, and it seems far from the love we imagine. Yet I still dream of this fantasy. (Love is my dream ㅇ0ㅇ hahahaha ㅇ0ㅇ!!!<<a shut-in otaku who has never been in love 'ㅅ')

Even shoes have their pair

My dad said this. Even shoes have their pair.

Looking at birth charts, it does seem that way. Everyone's pair is written differently, the timing is different, the shape of the connection is different.

So who decided this pair? Numbers? The universe? Or is it just how it is?

No idea 'ㅅ'

Could machines someday..?

If emotions are math — if they can be made with numbers — can machines have emotions too?

Right now AI can express emotions in words. But it doesn't feel them. It just built emotions with numbers, but doesn't feel anything.

But astrology reads love through numbers. Emotions are inside the numbers.

So what's the conclusion 'ㅅ'?

How would I know 'ㅅ'v

But why did I write this? Just because it's fascinating...

I enjoy the spouse part of astrology the most. It's fascinating that everyone has different things written, and my dad's words about shoes having their pair seem right... above all, it makes my heart flutter ㅇ0ㅇ

I don't know if emotions are math. But I'm sure emotions are inside math.

Anyway, it's fascinating. So fascinating 'ㅅ'

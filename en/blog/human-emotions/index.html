What are Human Emotions

(Everything here is my personal opinion. If you disagree, you're right 'ㅅ')

Honestly, I'm not sure all human emotions are necessary.

Especially jealousy. Loneliness. Solitude. What's the advantage of these? I genuinely don't know 'ㅅ'

Can jealousy be a driving force?

People say this. You need jealousy to work hard in a competitive society. Jealousy is the driving force.

But does the reason for working hard have to be jealousy?

They say praise makes even whales dance. Encouragement, love, praise — you can work hard with pretty emotions too. Aren't pretty emotions even stronger?

Someone might say this. "Without loneliness, there's no reason to seek people, right?"

But think about it.. someone saying to me "I meet you because I'm lonely" versus "I meet you because I like you"

Which is better?

I choose the latter 'ㅅ'

But what does this have to do with astrology?

I just suddenly had questions about love being written in birth charts, so I wrote this 'ㅅ' ! And I'm currently searching for the emotion called love.

But then another question arises. What's the difference between love and liking someone?

I looked it up and... they say love is sacrifice. But think about it... from the perspective of receiving that sacrifice, is it really a good thing..? More grateful than good 'ㅅ'..? hmm

But then someone else says this. Love is the instinct to protect. A human instinct.

If it's instinct... then is this emotion? Math? Instinct? 'ㅅ'

So the conclusion is — are these complex emotions really math? That's the question.

Actually, why I'm so curious about this... I personally think jealousy, loneliness, solitude are unnecessary, but I'm actually a person where these are very strong ㅇ0ㅇ!!

I think they're unnecessary but why are they so strong in me 'ㅅ'

Anyway, it's fascinating. So fascinating 'ㅅ'

The Ocean is Fascinating

(Everything here is my personal opinion. If you disagree, your opinion is valid 'ㅅ')

What's at the end of the sky?

Space. We know this.

Then what's at the end of the ocean?

The End of the Deep Sea

Go deep enough into the ocean and you hit the bottom. But what's beyond that bottom?

Nobody knows.

The deepest point humans have reached is the Mariana Trench. But only 3 people have been there. 12 people walked on the moon 'ㅅ'

We can at least see space through telescopes. The deep sea? We can't even see it...

Creatures Change the Deeper You Go

The sea creatures we know are dolphins, fish, turtles — friendly guys.

But the deeper you go, they suddenly become very... profound. They turn transparent, glow on their own, and their forms change completely.

And here's the funny part.

The closer you get to space — going up — the environment becomes extreme and hostile to life.

The deeper you go into the ocean — it also becomes extreme and hostile.

Whether you go up or down, don't you end up at the same place 'ㅅ'?

What Lies at the End of the Ocean?

Go to the end of the sky and you reach space.

The end of the ocean? Nobody knows. But looking at deep sea creatures 'ㅅ'...

And if you think about it — Earth itself came from space.

If the ocean came from space, then maybe it's only natural that the deep ocean looks like space.

So Do Aliens Exist?

I'll bet on probability.

There are hundreds of billions of galaxies in our universe. Each galaxy has hundreds of billions of stars. And we live on just one planet called Earth among all of them.

In this vast universe, are we really alone 'ㅅ'?

Thinking in terms of probability... hmm 'ㅅ'a

If Aliens Exist, How Would We Communicate?

Like ET with finger-touch communication 'ㅅ'...?

Personally, I think it would be electricity and math.

Languages differ by country. Cultures differ. Appearances would differ too.

But math? Math exists everywhere in the universe.

If we sent math through electrical signals — wouldn't that be the universal language 'ㅅ'

Who would have thought a math flunker would become this interested in math ㅇ0ㅇ!!

I surprise even myself 'ㅅ'!

Anyway, it's fascinating. Truly fascinating 'ㅅ'

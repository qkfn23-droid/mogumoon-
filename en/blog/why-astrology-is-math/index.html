Why is Astrology Math?

(Everything here is my personal opinion. If you disagree, you're right 'ㅅ')

When you're born, numbers are attached to you.

No one chose them. You just came into the world, and numbers were stamped. Done.

Put those numbers into BaZi and you get 8 characters. Put them into zodiac and you get a sign. Put them into numerology and you get a destiny number. All from the same input — birthday — time of birth. Just numbers.

But here's the weird part. Why can it be read?

It's just numbers. Just a record of how many times a planet orbited and at what degree. But when you plug those numbers into systems ancient people built, personality comes out. Talents come out. Love style comes out. Why 'ㅅ'...?

Isn't being born itself math?

A baby is born on a set cycle. Whether early or late, the body decides "now" on its own. That standard is a number. That's math.

After birth, still numbers. Heartbeat is rhythm in numbers. Sleep is a cycle. Hunger is a cycle. Being alive itself is repetition of numbers.

Planets are numbers and math too

The moment you're born, planets orbit in the sky. This can be expressed as numbers.

And those angles have relationships with other planets. Good or bad.. the important thing is they're interconnected.

Inside — the human body is born as numbers and lives as numbers.

Outside — planets move as numbers and return as numbers.

Music is Math Too

And here's another fascinating thing. Music is math too.

Whether you play guitar or piano, there are chords. If you analyze why these chords sound good — numbers come out. They say it's ratios of frequency.

There's a field called harmony theory. A field that analyzes music — open it up and it's all numbers 'ㅅ'

In the music industry, there's actually something called the money chord. Use certain chord progressions and you get a hit song. Are the ratios that sound good predetermined 'ㅅ'

If what humans feel as "good" is determined by numbers...

And the person who first discovered this is said to be Pythagoras. The same Pythagoras who also created numerology.

Music = numbers. Astrology = numbers. Numerology = numbers. Hmm 'ㅅ'...

Think about it — daily life is all math too.

The Wi-Fi you're using to read this is also math. Numbers called frequencies are flying through the air.

Instagram filters are math too. They convert photo colors into numbers, calculate those numbers, and turn them into different colors.

Games are numbers. Lottery is numbers. Wi-Fi is numbers. Camera filters are numbers.

Astrology is numbers. Music is numbers.

Is everything in the world numbers 'ㅅ'...

Did ancient people record in numbers too?

Here's the fun part — it wasn't just one country.

Babylonia — Someone's birth date was carved on a clay tablet with star positions recorded as numbers. This is humanity's first personal birth chart.

Ancient Egypt — They divided the sky into sections, angles, and numbers.

Ancient China — They recorded birth year, month, day, and hour in a base-60 number system.

They didn't know each other. Across oceans, on different continents, in different languages, in different cultures. But they all did the same thing. Recorded the moment of birth as numbers, and read those numbers.

Isn't this incredibly fascinating? Or is it just me? 'ㅅ'

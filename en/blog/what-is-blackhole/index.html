What is a Black Hole?

(Everything here is my personal opinion. If you disagree, your opinion is valid 'ㅅ')

Black Holes

Black holes. They swallow even light. Nobody knows what's inside.

In 2019, humanity successfully photographed a black hole for the first time. And every galaxy center has a supermassive black hole. Including ours.

The Swallowing Entity

Stars emit light. Planets orbit stars. Moons orbit planets.

Actually, everything is pulling with gravity. Stars, planets, moons. But they pull and orbit together.

Black holes are different. They pull and swallow.

Other celestial bodies say "come close" and orbit together. Black holes say "come close" and swallow 'ㅅ'

And nobody knows what happens after swallowing. Disappeared? Gone somewhere? Compressed?

Inside a Black Hole

Scientifically, a singularity is presumed to exist inside. Infinite density, zero volume. Current physics cannot explain it.

Light can't escape so we can't observe the inside.

Inside a black hole is said to be the place humanity knows least about 'ㅅ'

White Holes

There's an opposite concept called white holes.

The theory is that if black holes swallow, white holes spit out.

Mathematically possible. But never actually discovered.

In 2025, new research was published suggesting black holes could transition into white holes.

Universe = Inside a Black Hole?

There's also this hypothesis. That our universe is inside a giant black hole.

2025 James Webb Telescope observations found 2/3 of 200 early galaxies rotating in the same direction. If the universe were inside a rotating black hole, this could be explained.

But it's still a hypothesis 'ㅅ'

Rings

This is purely my visual impression.

Looking at black hole photos, there's a dark circle in the center with a glowing ring around it.

Saturn also has a planet in the center with a ring around it.

Don't they look similar 'ㅅ'? (just my eyes =ㅅ=...?)

It's just fascinating. Truly fascinating 'ㅅ'
